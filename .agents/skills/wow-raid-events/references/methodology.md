# Research workflow

Define a falsifiable question and metric before choosing tools. Inventory logs, route data, player inputs, SimC source/binary versions and reports. Record what is unavailable.

For route work, reconstruct the spawn ledger and pull table first, choose a model with explicit assumptions, then generate and validate the overlay. For implementation work, identify the active effect/action and follow the full execution chain. Preserve observations separately from interpretations.

Make a minimal baseline and change one factor. First verify event order and identities; then measure the relevant quantities across a statistical series. Preserve artifact hashes, settings, units, owners and aggregation. Explain sensitivity to model assumptions rather than reporting one unexplained DPS value.

Conclude with what the evidence establishes, its scope and remaining uncertainty. When competing hypotheses remain, propose a control for which they predict different outcomes. See [evidence rules](evidence-rules.md), [reproductions](minimal-repros.md) and [maintenance](knowledge-maintenance.md).
